
# Millworks Technologies Limited

## TL;DR
- First earnings call as a listed company following an IPO in July subscribed 219.54 times.
- H1 FY27 revenue from operations stood at ₹17.37 crores, with a net loss of ₹1.83 crores due to revenue recognition timing, deferred defense approvals, and fixed operating costs.
- Order book stands at ₹230 crores, including a record export order of ₹111 crores.
- Auditor issued a qualified opinion regarding trade receivables outstanding past 6 months (including ₹86.88 crores from Quick Pay Private Limited) and advances given for component procurement.

## Management Commentary
- The business is traditionally weighted towards the second half (H2), with approximately 70% of last year's revenue coming in H2.
- Revenue recognition was impacted by timing; ₹30 crores of orders executed in September fell into H2 due to accounting standards and October timing.
- Defense approvals and inspections took longer than expected, moving planned H1 orders into H2.
- Management prioritized paying suppliers despite customer payment delays to maintain strong partnerships.
- Made Vidwan Aeronautics a subsidiary, set up a 3D printing center of excellence with Markforged (USA), signed an MoU with TridenTech to co-develop UAV engines, and is undergoing audits to transition from Tier 2 to Tier 1.
- Intends to balance Indian defense orders with export defense orders, putting more focus on exports for faster cash flows and advance payments.
- Shifting focus from build-to-print to build-to-spec (aiming for a 50/50 mix or up to 70% build-to-spec within two years) to secure better margins.
- Export payment terms updated to require a 30% advance with purchase orders and 30 to 45 days payment terms from invoicing.

## Guidance and Outlook
- Confident of a significantly stronger second half (H2 FY27) driven by deferred revenue recognition, new capacity utilization, and healthy order book.
- Expected to realize pending receivable amounts in the third quarter / December.
- Full-year FY27 revenue expected to cross INR 200 crores.
- Projected year-on-year growth of 60% to 70% for FY28, driven by First Article Samples converting into production orders.
- Total order execution and completion of the ₹111 crore export order will take 12 to 14 months (expected by the end of next financial year).

## Segment Performance
- Export share accounted for over a quarter of revenue last year, strengthened by a new ₹111 crore export order.
- Current build-to-print vs build-to-spec mix is 70/30, with build-to-spec carrying 3% to 5% higher margins.
- Current revenue contribution ranking: 1) Defense, 2) Rail, 3) Aerospace, 4) Semiconductor machinery parts (management aims to move Aerospace ahead of Rail).

## Risks and Concerns
- Raw material prices driven by geopolitical situations compressed gross margins modestly.
- Defense approvals and inspections are unpredictable, causing revenue shifts between halves.
- Fixed costs (employee, finance, depreciation) ran in full during H1 against a light revenue period, resulting in a net loss.
- Government of India defense processes are manual and time-consuming, resulting in longer cash realization cycles.
- Build-to-spec involves a longer gestation period (3 to 6 months), significant engineering time investment, and specification changes.
- Auditor's qualified opinion regarding INR 86.88 crores outstanding from Quick Pay for 6-8 months, an INR 10.59 crore advance, and lack of timely receipts/documentation for defense orders.
- Potential conflict with RBI regulations requiring export order realizations within 181 days due to large amounts outstanding past 6 months.
