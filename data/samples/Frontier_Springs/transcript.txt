Revenue from operations for the 2nd Quarter came in at Rs. 99.73 crores up by 20.54% 
from Rs. 82.74 crores in Quarter 2 of FY26 and up 27.11% from Rs. 78.46 crores in 
Quarter 1 of FY27.  
EBITDA for the quarter stood at Rs. 29.73 crores, a growth of 34.69% year-on-year. 
Our EBITDA margin was 29.81% and improvement of 313 basis points year-on-year 
and 529 basis points over the previous quarter. 
Finance cost remained negligible at Rs. 0.04 crores and depreciation was Rs. 1.30 
crores. Profit before tax was Rs. 28.5 crores up by 35.19% and profit after tax for the 
quarter came in at Rs. 21.33 crores up by 35.79% year-on-year and 77.57% over the 
previous quarter. 
For the first half, Financial Year-’27: Revenue from operations was Rs. 178.19 crores 
up by 12.72% from Rs. 158.08 crores. In H1 FY26. EBITDA was Rs. 48.97 crores up by 
15.23% with a margin of 27.48% and improvement of 60 basis points. Profit after tax 
for the half year was Rs. 33.34 crores up 9.48%. 
The half-year numbers carry the effect of the 1st Quarter and the 2nd Quarter shows 
the business back on track. Our balance sheet remains strong cash flow from 
operating activities for the half year was Rs. 63.48 crores compared to Rs. 31.41 
crores for the whole Financial Year-‘26. Trade receivable came down to Rs. 42.97 
crores from Rs. 60.89 crores at the end of March. Total borrowings stood at Rs. 5.35 
crores against cash and bank balances of Rs. 32.73 crores. 
On the raw material side, raw material availability was constrained by the geopolitical 
situation and we could not complete some of the orders we had planned to execute. 
We executed those orders in the 2nd Quarter and that is part of the growth you see in 
the quarter numbers. Looking ahead, steel, oil and gas prices are higher and this could 
put some pressure on margins in the coming quarter. 
 
We are factoring current input costs into our tender bids to protect profitability. We 
are watching this closely and we will manage it actively. Turning to the business 
updates, on Coil Springs which has been our mainstream for over 3 decades, we 
continue to see steady demand across freight stock, coaching stock and locomotive 
applications. 
 
Our springs continue to be fitted on Vande Bharat Express. Tenders for 4,000 wagons 
have now been finalized and these will drive the next leg of growth for the segment. 

 
 
Page 4 of 15 
Our air spring business for LHB coaches continues to perform strongly. This is a 
business we have been patiently building since 2022. We are now running at around 
250 coaches every month. Forgings orders continues to flow in. The growth this 
quarter was driven entirely by the railways to raise capacity utilization. 
We have also taken up job work for automotive companies. Margins on automotive 
work are slightly lower but the additional volume improves utilization and brings 
down the operating costs of our forging capacity which supports growth in this 
division also. 
 
On FIBA, our Failure Indication and Brake Application system, I have said on the last 
call that it remained on track. I am pleased to report that we have received RDSO 
approval and we expect to get trial order shortly. The trial run will be for 6 months to 
8 months after which we expect commercial production to begin. 
 
Across our verticals, demand remains strong and our order position continues to be 
robust. We expect to maintain this run rate. On our outlook for the year, on our last 
call we spoke of crossing Rs. 500 crores in gross revenue, a financial rate of Rs. 27 
crores. Supply constraints in the 1st Quarter set us back and we now expect Rs. 27 
crores gross revenue of at least Rs. 450 crores. We continue to push hard on 
execution and are working towards our stated guidance of Rs. 500 crores. In closing, 
the 2nd Quarter has shown what this business can do when supply is in the place. 
 
The Indian Railways growth story is well-funded and multi-tiered in nature. We are 
positioned to ride this tailwind as a trusted, certified manufacturer with over 40 years 
of expertise, an expanding product portfolio and the balance sheet that gives us the 
flexibility to invest. I look forward to your questions.
